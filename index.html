What is this?

Slothcard makes a single, shareable SVG postcard. Everything runs in your browser; the “share link” just encodes your postcard settings in the URL.

  • No login, no tracking pixels, no API keys.
  • Works offline after first load.
  • Export as SVG (crisp) or PNG (easy).

Privacy

Your message stays in the URL you share. If you want it private, don’t share the link.
